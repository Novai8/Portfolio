I woke up this morning, and something was seriously wrong with the world.
A bird was flying backward.
The rain was falling up into the sky.
And my neighbor had been standing in the exact same position for three hours, with one leg inside a wall.
I thought I was losing my mind.
Then I tried to open my front door.
It led to the middle of the ocean.
I slammed it shut, opened it again, and suddenly I was standing in a grocery store.
Every single item had my face on it.
Even the milk.
I decided the smartest thing to do was stay inside and wait for reality to fix itself.
Terrible decision.
At exactly noon, the sky turned blue and a giant error message appeared above the city.
"REALITY UPDATE FAILED. RESTARTING IN 100 DAYS."
Then everyone froze.
Everyone except me.
I walked outside and waved my hand in front of a frozen police officer. Nothing.
I threw a rock at a car, and the rock turned into a chicken halfway through the air.
I was about to celebrate my new life as the world's most powerful idiot when I noticed something across the street.
A second stickman.
He was moving, too.
He stared directly at me, pointed toward the sky, and mouthed three words.
"You're not real."
Then he disappeared.
A countdown appeared above my head.
99 days, 23 hours, 59 minutes.
And suddenly, I had a much bigger problem than backward birds and chicken rocks.
I had to figure out who created this world before it restarted.
Because something tells me the next update isn't going to be pretty.
So, what should I do first?
A: Break into the mysterious building where the countdown began.
B: Find the stickman who said I'm not real.
Choose carefully.
We've got 100 days to figure out what the hell is happening.
