**Episode:** Day 2

**Subtitle policy:** full narration burned in; the two in-world scratched messages and the two choice cards are on-screen typography, not subtitles (cues 9, 12, 36, 37 are suppressed from the subtitle track)

## Scene 1 — The Search Begins (0.00 – 16.36) · cues 0–3
- **Beat:** Recap of Day 1; the decision to hunt the mystery stickman; he has already vanished.
- **Visuals:** Hero (small, h≈430) on a dark rooftop/street edge under a vast skyline. Wrist timer always reads **98D 11:42:06**. Subtle glitch shimmer on distant windows; a faint magenta after-image of where the mystery figure stood, dissolving in the first 3 s.
- **Camera/motion:** slow push-in; two micro glitch-jitters synced to cues 1–2.
- **Cue 3** ("vanished into thin air"): the magenta after-image tears apart into particles and the frame does a single horizontal slice tear into Scene 2.

## Scene 2 — Six-Hour Montage (16.36 – 34.01) · cues 4–7
- **Beat:** Searching the city; anomalies cluster around the hero.
- **Visuals:** Three rotating street vignettes inside one continuous walk:
  1. **Flickering streetlight (cue 5):** sodium lamp strobes only as the hero passes beneath it.
  2. **Clocks at 11:11 (cue 6):** every clock face — wrist, wall, shop sign — reads 11:11; a storefront OPEN sign hangs high and clear of the sidewalk.
  3. **Apartment number on every door (cue 7):** a receding corridor of identical doors, each bearing the hero's own apartment number.
- **Citizens:** looping background NPCs (coffee cup, phone, steps, cyclist) perform one action and freeze/glitch when the hero looks at them. No on-screen labels.
- **Motion:** lateral parallax walk cycle, footsteps matching the foley; glitch chirps punctuate each anomaly.

## Scene 3 — First Warning / The Alley (34.01 – 50.79) · cues 8–13
- **Beat:** The scratched warning, then a second message ordering him to look up.
- **Visuals:** Narrow wet-walled alley, single dim overhead fixture with a tight light pool (no wide light dome). Stone wall with chips and pinned notices.
  - **Cue 9 — "STOP LOOKING FOR ME":** text is *carved into the stone* — recessed dark groove, pale stone face, bright chipped upper edge, dust particles falling as it appears. A dark blurred plate behind the text zone keeps the carving legible.
  - **Cue 12 — "LOOK UP":** a second, smaller carving materializes beneath the first.
- **Camera:** slow tilt upward begins on cue 13 ("So I did") and the frame tear at 50.79 reveals the impossible building.

## Scene 4 — The Impossible Building (50.79 – 57.43) · cue 14
- **Beat:** The mystery stickman stands atop a building that materializes out of nothing.
- **Visuals:** A tall slab assembles itself from descending wireframe blocks and glitched windows; the hero stands small at its base. The mystery figure is clearly visible standing on the roof line (≈ y268), correctly proportioned — never a giant. A low materialization groan + cyan/magenta settle glitches.

## Scene 5 — The Impossible Staircase (57.43 – 63.50) · cue 15
- **Beat:** Running upstairs; the staircase changes direction under him; he nearly falls.
- **Visuals:** Four perspective steps that rotate/snap their direction mid-climb, a railing that rattles, and a snap-back lurch where the hero stumbles but catches himself. Handheld-style shake on the two mechanical shifts.

## Scene 6 — Rooftop Confrontation (63.50 – 109.80) · cues 16–33
The dramatic core. Mystery stickman (cyan rim, h≈420) faces the hero (h≈430) on the rooftop with the city far below.
- **Cues 16–19 — "He was waiting… Who are you?"** establishing two-shot, rooftop door and HVAC props framing them.
- **Cue 19 — "I'm the reason you're still alive."** close two-shot, cyan rim pulse.
- **Cues 20–24 — The countdown:** he grabs the hero's arm; holographic countdown appears above the hero's head (matching his wrist, 98 days) while the mystery figure raises his own wrist. **His timer reads 00:00:00** — explicitly *his* timer; the hero's stays at 98 days. Two activation pulses; a settle-distortion on "It said zero."
- **Cues 25–26 — Sky fracture:** near-vertical branching cyan cracks and displaced rectangular sky fragments spread across the sky only (clipped above the roofline, so they never form a humanoid silhouette or touch the characters).
- **Cues 27–28 — "…the world doesn't restart. YOU do.":** held close two-shot; a single restrained low impact lands on "YOU do."
- **Cue 29 — The city turns:** cutaway to a wide row of five citizens whose heads rotate **in exact synchronization**, each head flaring cyan as it locks toward the rooftop; one cup-holder close-up and one cyclist close-up inside the beat, returning to the wide turned row.
- **Cues 30–31 — The key:** the mystery figure presses a small self-centering glowing key into the hero's palm (clink + resonant tone); "Find the room with no door."
- **Cue 32 — Don't trust tomorrow:** brief stereo phase instability to underscore the warning.
- **Cue 33 — Disappearance:** tight figure-bounded segment disintegration (six vertical strips, ≤26 px displacement, alpha fade, no full-frame bands and no crossing streak), a digital tear, and a low drop. The rooftop is left empty; the hero remains.

## Scene 7 — Isolation / The Monologue (109.80 – 118.35) · cue 34
- **Beat:** A key that opens nothing; a city staring at him; the weight of being watched.
- **Visuals:** Hero alone on the rooftop, the key faint in his hand at 80% opacity, the turned city glowing below. Slow orbit; wind dominates; the drone swells. Ends on a hard cut at 118.35.

## Scene 8 — The Choice (118.35 – 130.04) · cues 35–38
- **Beat:** Direct-to-camera question, then two branching end cards.
- **Cue 35 — "So what do I do?"** hero centered, timer still 98D 11:42:06.
- **Choice card A (cyan, door glyph):** "SEARCH FOR THE ROOM WITH NO DOOR"
- **Choice card B (magenta, eye glyph):** "FIND OUT WHY EVERYONE IS WATCHING ME"
- Cards are stacked, non-overlapping, with letter badges A/B and ample margin; each gets its own UI pulse.
- **Cue 38:** pull back over the watching city as the final digital tear resolves into a clean (non-frozen) dark end frame. No black frames anywhere in the episode; the ending holds the choice cards, not a black screen.
